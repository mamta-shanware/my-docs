# How to Schedule a Message in Slack

Scheduling a message allows you to draft a communication and automatically send it to a colleague or channel at a specific *future date and time*.

### Prerequisites
* You must have a registered Slack account.
* You must be logged into your workspace on a computer or mobile app.

### Steps
1. Open the specific channel or direct message conversation where you want to send the text.
2. Type your message into the text box, but *do not* press the Send button.
3. Click the down arrow next to the send icon to select the **Schedule for later** option. 
4. Select **Custom time** and pick your desired day and time using the dropdown menus.
5. Click the **Schedule Message** button to finalize your message queue.
